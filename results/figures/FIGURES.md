# Figures

Regenerate with `python src/figures.py --out results/figures` (needs `matplotlib`; it reads only committed result CSVs). PDF (vector, fonts embedded) and PNG (300 dpi), sized for an IEEE two-column page (7.16 in). Colours are Okabe–Ito, and every series also differs in marker, fill or line style. Bootstrap intervals use seed 20261124.
